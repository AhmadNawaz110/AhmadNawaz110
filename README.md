## About

I am a cybersecurity researcher and university lecturer working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and security. My research focuses on building practical, privacy-aware, and explainable systems for cyber resilience, with interests spanning AI-driven cybersecurity, privacy-preserving machine learning, digital identity security, explainable AI, and blockchain.

Alongside research, I have more than 8 years of combined teaching and industry experience. I currently serve as a Cybersecurity Lecturer at the Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology (GIKI) and as a Senior Cybersecurity Specialist at Kawai Enterprise Solution. I have also provided consultancy for national-level initiatives involving NADRA and the KP Forensic Science Lab.

**Researching** — AI-driven cybersecurity, privacy-preserving data publishing, explainable intrusion detection, federated anomaly detection, and digital identity security  
**Building** — AI-powered security testing tools, explainable IDS pipelines, DevSecOps monitoring systems, and blockchain-based registries  
**Teaching** — Cryptography, AI/ML for cybersecurity, blockchain, DevSecOps, malware analysis, digital forensics, VAPT, network security, OOP, and data structures  
**Serving** — Faculty advisor for 40+ undergraduate students and consultant for public-sector cybersecurity and forensic programs  
**Currently** — Working on privacy-preserving federated anomaly detection and adversarial robustness benchmarking for explainable AI-driven IDS
