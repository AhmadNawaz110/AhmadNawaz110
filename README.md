Research Identity & PhD Intent
The Grand Challenge: As AI systems embed themselves into critical national infrastructure—identity management, counter-terrorism networks, healthcare data pipelines, and wireless communication backbones—the threat surface expands across every engineering layer simultaneously. No existing framework addresses physical-layer wireless vulnerabilities, formal privacy guarantees for dynamic multi-attribute datasets, adversarial robust AI decision systems, and blockchain-verified forensic integrity as a unified research program. My work is building that framework, layer by layer, from silicon to intelligence.
Research Lineage: My BS thesis established physical-layer expertise—a 2×2 MIMO-OFDM system implemented on NI-USRP hardware, analyzing BER/MSE under Rayleigh fading and AWGN channels, evaluating MMSE equalization for ISI suppression. This hardware-to-algorithm depth is the foundation. My MS thesis (D-MSA) formalized the failure modes of static privacy models under dynamic multi-attribute healthcare data, proposing enhanced p⁺-sensitive k-anonymity with cutting-and-slicing defending simultaneously against four distinct attack classes. Government deployments (NADRA, CTD KP Police) provided operational validation no other researcher at my career stage possesses.
Aim: To establish Pakistan's first interdisciplinary deep-tech security research lab bridging Electrical Engineering, AI/ML, Quantum-Safe Cryptography, Wireless Security, and Digital Forensics to produce globally publishable research and train the next generation of Pakistani research scientists and security engineers.
